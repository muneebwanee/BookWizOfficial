# The idea behind BookWiz

**One place for a book to become itself.**

---

I wanted the planning desk, manuscript, story notebook, and publishing table to feel like parts of the same room. BookWiz brings them together without putting the writer's library on a server.

## The complete arc

**Imagine → Plan → Draft → Revise → Design → Export**

The library is the starting point. Each book carries its own brief, outline, chapters, characters, worlds, notes, and cover. Changes to the story guide inform future drafting, while existing prose remains the writer's to review.

## What matters

- **A private desk.** Local storage, independent backups, and no BookWiz account.
- **An attentive assistant.** Optional model connections, explicit sharing approval, and editable proposals.
- **A coherent object.** Front matter, typography, physical trim, covers, and exports belong to one book.
- **A comfortable room.** Readable type, measured motion, device-aware controls, and an uncluttered focus view.

The software serves the author's choices. It does not claim the author's work, take a royalty, or promise a publishing outcome.
