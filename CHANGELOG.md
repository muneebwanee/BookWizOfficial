## 1.0.1 · Mobile layouts, native PDF and home-screen shortcuts

- Android widgets now have distinct layouts and grid sizes, with a clock, writing shortcuts and weekly progress. Book tiles restore their saved chapter; other tiles open the controls they name.
- Mobile library list rows keep their covers and titles aligned. Draft Flow labels fit and the final controls scroll clear of navigation.
- Cover selection and book proofs keep their controls visible; proofs default to fitting the complete page.
- Literary polish opens a visible text-model chooser when needed and produces revisions for review after sharing approval.
- Mobile PDF options use the device’s print engine. Android requests the book’s trim, avoids extra system margins and embeds the local fonts.
- A shared automatic patch version advances on source updates; all editions build from one immutable version tag. Missing active workflow definitions are restored, and star-history pushes retry concurrent uploads. Source pushes automatically prepare and publish all editions. All eight source ZIPs, platform READMEs, checksums, origin metadata and the offline cache refresh automatically, without duplicate release builds. ZIP-only source distributions retain verified extraction.

## 1.0.0

### The writing room

- A local library with three original reading samples and JSON backups for moving your own work.
- A guided book brief, editable chapter outline, character sheets, world notes and quick capture.
- Rich manuscript editing with focus mode, word counts, find and replace, undo/redo and saved versions.
- Draft Flow with streaming continuation previews, phrase pins, live steering, a timeline playhead, saved alternatives and guarded chapter insertion.
- The Red Pen for close reading and optional model suggestions that stay under the writer's control.
- Writing sessions, word goals, read-aloud and ten light/dark theme collections.

### A book you can carry out

- 32 cover directions with editable typography, color and composition.
- Trim-aware page proofs and independent EPUB, Markdown, JSON, print/PDF and LaTeX source exports.
- Optional local Unicode typesetting through a compatible compiler.

### On your devices

- A responsive shared studio for web, Android, iPhone/iPad and desktop hosts.
- A universal Mac application, a combined Windows installer, Linux packages, Android APK/AAB output and Apple project sources.
- Widgets, local writing reminders, file controls and speech on supported mobile editions.
- An Apple Watch companion for captured thoughts and progress, and an Apple TV reading room with local book transfer.

- Citation metadata for BookWiz by muneebwanee, available through GitHub’s Cite this repository action.

### Connections and privacy

- Optional hosted providers and compatible endpoints, with explicit sharing approval.
- Automatic local-model discovery for running Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp services.
- Apple Intelligence on compatible iPhone/iPad devices when the device model is available.
- Locally bundled fonts, provider marks and application assets; no BookWiz account or application telemetry.
