<details>
<summary><strong>About these images</strong></summary>

The 35 writing-studio scenes are actual browser captures of BookWiz’s shared interface, presented inside illustrative device frames. The ten Apple Watch and Apple TV scenes are source-faithful native-interface previews reconstructed from their SwiftUI layouts. They show the companion features available on those platforms.

Book titles, authors, word counts, model connection labels, continuation previews, transfer addresses and codes are illustrative sample data. Android is shown in **Ocean dark** and iPhone in **Ivory light**; these are selected BookWiz themes.

The five studio scenes cover the library, Draft Flow continuation steering, character profiles, cover design and page proofs. Watch scenes cover progress, thought capture and delivery status. TV scenes cover its library, reader, outline, read-aloud and local JSON transfer.

The device frames are original vector illustrations based on the named hardware. Display and body proportions follow the corresponding model specifications; finishes, perspective and lighting are presentation artwork. The television is a generic 16:9 display connected to the separate Apple TV box. These are not product photographs or captures from physical devices.

The PNGs are ready to view without extra assets. [Device references and image details →](../docs/SHOWCASE.md)

Poster typography: [Anton](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton) and [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans), published under the SIL Open Font License.

</details>
