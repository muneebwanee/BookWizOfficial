Build this edition directly from a clone of BookWizOfficial, using the repository root that contains `package.json`. The optional `source-watchos.zip` snapshot also contains a self-contained edition for people who prefer an archive.

## A companion to your writing room

Capture a thought by typing or dictating, keep notes queued until the iPhone is available, and glance at writing progress. The Watch app belongs to the paired BookWiz iPhone application.

## Downloads

The companion is embedded in the **iPhone IPA**. It uses the signed paired app rather than a standalone phone installer. Device installation follows the iPhone app's signing and provisioning.

## This source edition

The modern single-target SwiftUI Watch app lives under `native/apple/Watch/`. The deployment target is watchOS 10. **BookWiz-iOS** embeds **BookWiz-Watch** and the Widgets extension. Source for all related Apple targets is retained in this archive.
