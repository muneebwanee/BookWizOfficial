# Find the next turn.

**Draft Flow · A little more room to explore a sentence.**

[BookWiz](../README.md) · [Connect a model](PROVIDERS.md) · [Your first book](GETTING_STARTED.md)

</div>

---

Open a book in **Writing studio**, then choose **Draft Flow**. Your chapter stays in view while you explore a possible continuation beside its direction controls. Nothing is added to the manuscript until you choose **Keep preview**.

## Shape a direction

Write a short intention for the next passage. A line such as *Let Ada recognise the instrument before anyone speaks* gives the model a useful place to begin.

Three controls shape the writing request:

| Control | One end | The other |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pace** | Reflective, with room for a thought. | Brisk, with more forward movement. |
| **Detail** | Spare and selective. | Immersive and richly observed. |
| **Dialogue** | Interior and descriptive. | Conversational, with more spoken exchange. |

These are directions for the prose, rather than promises about the exact result. Your selected book language, voice, chapter beat and story guide remain part of the request.

## Keep a detail close

Select a few words in the chapter or preview, then choose **Pin selected words**. You can also type a phrase into **Story pins**.

Keep up to eight names, objects or phrases in view. Each pin has an emphasis control and can be switched off without deleting it. Pinned phrases are highlighted when they appear in the displayed text. Pins guide the writing prompt; they do not measure or expose the model’s internal attention.

## Explore the page ahead

Choose **Explore a continuation** and approve the request. Compatible provider connections stream the words as they arrive. Device models and native local connections can return the completed preview as one passage.

Move **Explore from this point** to keep part of an uncommitted preview as the starting point for the next request. **Try another** asks for a different continuation. Earlier previews stay in the chapter’s history, so you can return to a direction that nearly worked.

**Keep preview** adds the entire displayed preview. **Keep to playhead** adds only the words before the selected point. A chapter version is saved before the addition, and later drafted chapters are flagged when a continuity check may be useful.

If the source chapter has changed, the earlier preview stays available to read or copy; explore again from the new ending before adding a passage. **Discard preview** removes only that proposal.

## Tune while the words arrive

Turn on **Live tuning** to refresh a preview after changing the direction, a pin, a slider or the playhead. Changes made close together are grouped into one request. A live session permits up to **six model requests**, then pauses. Previously saved directions can be revisited without another model request.

**Stop** pauses live tuning and keeps received preview text for review. Leaving Draft Flow stops its active preview request. A stopped provider request may already have used tokens.

## Your words stay yours

The feature uses the model connections already configured in BookWiz. Opening the workspace, pinning a phrase or adjusting controls with Live tuning off does not send manuscript text. Before the first model request, BookWiz asks permission to process the book brief, chapter excerpts, direction and pins for that session.

Draft Flow settings and up to eight previews are saved with the chapter on your device and included in BookWiz JSON backups. API keys remain in the existing session-key or encrypted-vault system. They are not stored in a preview or exported in a manuscript backup.

Draft Flow is available in the Android, iPhone/iPad, Windows, macOS, Linux and web writing studio. Apple Watch and Apple TV retain their companion interfaces.

## Small shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alt/Option Enter** | Explore a continuation. |
| **⌘/Ctrl Enter** | Keep the displayed preview. |
| **Escape** | Stop an active preview. |
| **⌘/Ctrl K**, then *Draft Flow* | Find the workspace through search. |
