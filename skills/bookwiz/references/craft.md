# Craft without a house style

## The scene engine

A scene has a particular person pursuing something under pressure. Identify what they want now, what resists them, what they try, what changes and what the change costs. A quiet observation can be a scene when it changes a relationship, belief or intention; action does not have to mean violence or spectacle.

Choose an entry close to the pressure. Let the setting affect what can happen. After a meaningful turn, allow a consequence to land rather than immediately explaining its lesson. Vary pace across the chapter: compression for routine, space for decisions and specific perception.

## Character and dialogue

A useful profile connects desire, need, fear, contradiction, relationships, habits, limits and voice. Give each field evidence from the actual story. A character may be opaque by design; do not diagnose them from a trope.

Dialogue is an attempt to do something: evade, secure, test, comfort, threaten, bargain or make contact. Leave room for subtext and unequal knowledge. Differentiate speakers through priorities, syntax and attention rather than decorative accents. Read aloud to catch identical rhythms and dialogue that exists only to explain the plot.

## Voice, language and viewpoint

Track person, tense, narrative distance, vocabulary, sentence rhythm, humor, sensory attention and what the narrator refuses to say. Respect code-switching, deliberate dialect and the chosen script. Preserve diacritics and correct right-to-left text. Broad influences can be translated into craft choices such as close third-person, spare description or associative transitions; do not simulate a living writer's signature voice.

## Details and imagery

Prefer an observed detail that changes the situation to a decorative inventory. Let metaphors come from the character's world. Repeated motifs gain meaning through changes in context, not through repeated explanation. Keep factual and physical constraints intact unless the book establishes another rule.

## Nonfiction movement

Each chapter advances a claim through evidence, examples, limitations and a usable takeaway. State uncertainty at the scale the evidence supports. Use examples that reveal a mechanism; avoid padding a short argument into an arbitrary target. Define technical terms when needed and distinguish a practical recommendation from a universal rule.

## A useful finish check

Read the last paragraph against the opening question. Has the scene or argument changed? Can any explanation be removed because the action or evidence already shows it? Does the next chapter inherit a real consequence? Keep an intentional ambiguity when it strengthens the work; ambiguity and confusion are different editorial findings.
