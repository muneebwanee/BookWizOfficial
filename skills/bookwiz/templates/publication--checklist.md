# Publication checklist

- [ ] Actual saved chapter order and word count reviewed
- [ ] Empty or partial chapters identified
- [ ] Title, subtitle, author and language correct
- [ ] Reading direction and script checked
- [ ] Supplied dedication, preface, imprint and rights statement reviewed
- [ ] Sources, quotations, permissions and unresolved factual claims reviewed
- [ ] Markdown/EPUB/backup opened and checked
- [ ] EPUB navigation, first/last chapter lines and special characters checked
- [ ] Editable sources and independent backup retained
- [ ] Printer or storefront requirements checked for the intended edition
